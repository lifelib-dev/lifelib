# Sources

Source ids, titles, publishers, URLs and retrieval markers are carried over **verbatim**
from `_research/dependance.md`, the citation ground truth for the [S#] and [R#] tags used in
`product-spec.md` and `technical-notes.md`. That numbering — S1–S12 and R1–R18 — is frozen
and is never renumbered here. Ids the two documents do not cite are omitted, leaving gaps:
**S12, R15, R16 and R17 are absent.** S12 is the Sogecap product, for which no *notice
d'information*, *conditions générales* or IPID could be located; every fact about it is cited
to [R12], the dissertation that describes it, so S12 is never used as a tag. R15 (ACPR,
lessons of its LTC supervisory controls), R16 (*Revue de l'ACPR*, September 2023) and R17
(DGCCRF *fiche pratique*) all returned HTTP 403 and the research file records no content
from any of them; the resulting absence of any ACPR view of this product is stated in
`product-spec.md`, *Regulatory context*, as the largest hole in this product's sourcing.

Access date for all citations: **2026-08-26**. No new sources were fetched at drafting.

Extraction details live in `_research/dependance.md`, which records which fact came from

published statements but not as policy terms; and the partial retrieval of the Code des
assurances [R18], which is why nothing about the *provision pour risques croissants* is cited
to the code.

Two absences shape everything marked **[std]** in the other two documents. **No public French
LTC incidence or continuance table exists** in these notes: [R12] specifies the structure of
the laws a model needs — incidence by age and sex, mortality in dependence by duration since
onset, and lapse — but its numerical bases are the insurer's own experience tables and are
not disclosed, and no BCAC-style published reference table for *dépendance* was located. And
**no rate card is public**: the only published premium scale found is [S5] annexe 1 for a
specific group contract, and the only other price points are the Groupama illustration [S8]
and the CCSF's 2013 indicative pricing [R8 §2.2](#frlib-dependance-r8), on which the worked configuration's

cause mix behind the *carence*, the severity shares that convert public GIR prevalence into
insured-state prevalence, and the tariff-revision path are therefore **[std]** constructions
introduced at drafting, attributable to no source, and each is footnoted where it appears.
