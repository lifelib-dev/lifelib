# Sources

Source ids [S#] / [R#] are carried verbatim from `_research/individual-annuity.md` (the
citation ground truth for this product) and are **frozen — never renumber**. Unused sources
are omitted, so the R-numbering has gaps: R1, R6, R7 and R8 of the research file are not
cited by the product documents, because the cross-product reference library carries the same
material in a fuller retrieval ([REG-R10] for 平成8年大蔵省告示第48号 and its reset mechanics,
[REG-R11] for the 2018 table adoption, [REG-R21] for the 日本アクチュアリー会 index and terms of use).
All fifteen primary sources are listed, including the three whose PDFs downloaded but could
not be text-extracted — they are cited exactly once, in the scope note of `product-spec.md`,
as the carriers that were attempted and lost, and no other fact in this product's documents
rests on them. Access date for all sources: 2026-08-20. No sources were newly added at
drafting. Cross-product [REG-R#] tags are listed in their own section at the end.

`references/regulatory-and-actuarial-references.md` (its own R-numbering, R1–R47, frozen;

annuity documents:

- **REG-R2** — 保険業法 第4条: 基礎書類, and why the 算出方法書 is unpublished — the reason every
  pricing-basis parameter here is **[std]** while 約款 facts carry [S#].
- **REG-R4** — 保険業法 第116条: the 責任準備金 obligation and the 標準責任準備金 delegation.
- **REG-R6** — 保険業法 第121条: the 保険計理人's 意見書, the statutory demand behind the 1号収支分析.
- **REG-R7** — 保険業法施行規則 第68条: which contracts are standard-reserve contracts.
- **REG-R8** — 保険業法施行規則 第69条: the 保険料積立金 / 未経過保険料 / 払戻積立金 / 危険準備金 taxonomy.
- **REG-R9** — 保険業法施行規則 第30条の2: the four permitted surplus-distribution methods, and the
  honest limit that 三利源 is practice vocabulary, not regulatory language.
- **REG-R10** — 平成8年大蔵省告示第48号: 平準純保険料式, the table vintages by contract date, and every 標準利率
  reset rule.

  the 2007 table — the asymmetry this product's two phases sit on.
- **REG-R12** — 告示改正 (2021): the extension of 標準責任準備金 to USD- and AUD-denominated contracts,
